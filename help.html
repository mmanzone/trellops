Getting started and privacy

Signing in

Sign in with your existing Atlassian Trello account. Trello will ask you to allow Trellops to access your boards.

Privacy and permissions

  • Local storage: Trellops follows a “local first” policy. The settings you enter stay in your browser, not on our servers.
  • Read and write access: By default Trellops only needs read access to show your dashboard. If you want to move cards between lists from the Map view, you will be asked to sign in again and allow write access.
  • Shared links: If you share your configuration as a link, we store the skeleton of your layout (board IDs and block names) so the link works. No card content is stored.

Setting up your dashboard

Once you have picked a board, open Settings. It has three tabs: Dashboard, Map and Other.

Creating blocks

A block is a group of list tiles. Blocks let you organise the board the way your team thinks about it, for example “Backlog”, “Sprint” and “Completed”, rather than in Trello’s order.

  • Create and name: Create as many blocks as you need and give them meaningful names.
  • Hide and show: Hide a whole block from the dashboard when you don’t need it, such as a “Done” column while you focus on active work.

Configuring tiles

Each Trello list appears as a tile. Use drag and drop to organise them:

  • Assigning lists: Drag a list from the “Unassigned” area into any block.
  • Reordering: Want “Urgent” first? Drag the tile up or down.
  • Colours: Click a tile to give it a colour, such as red for bugs or green for finished work. Tiles without a colour get one picked for them.

Using the Map view

See your work geographically. Trellops plots cards on a map using the location in each card.

How it works

Trellops looks for a location in two ways:

  1. Coordinates (preferred): If a card has a latitude and longitude, it is plotted straight away.
  2. Address lookup: Otherwise Trellops reads the card’s description. If it finds an address, it uses Google Maps to locate it.
The first load can take a moment. Google Maps limits how many addresses can be looked up at once, so a map with 50 new addresses may take a while to fill in. Locations are then cached in your browser, so the next load is instant.

Map features

  • Custom icons: In the Map settings tab, give each block its own icon.
  • Icons by label: Show a specific icon for cards with a given Trello label, such as a fire icon for anything labelled “Urgent”.
  • Home address: Set a home address to mark your office or headquarters on the map.
  • Move cards: Move a card to another list directly from the map (requires write access).
  • Street View (optional): Turn on “Show street view” in Map settings and card popups will include a Google Street View link for the card’s address.
  • Fit map: The “Fit map” button frames all your pins. With a single pin, it zooms to a comfortable street level instead of maximum zoom.

Advanced settings and filters

Lists that start with an instructions card

Sometimes the first card in a list isn’t a task but a set of instructions or a header, which makes the count wrong. Fix this in the tile’s Advanced settings:

  • Don’t count first card: Ignores the first card so the total reflects real work.
  • Display as description: Shows that first card’s text on the tile, as a header or instruction for your team.
  • Ignore keywords: In Settings › Other, enter comma-separated keywords. Cards with any of them in their title are left out of the dashboard, map and statistics.

Your own terminology

If “Board” or “Card” isn’t how your team talks, open Settings › Other and find Naming config. Rename the terms (for example “Card” to “Task”, or “Label” to “Tag”) and the new words are used across the dashboard and statistics.

Statistics and AI summaries (beta)

Open the /stats page to see activity over any period. The AI summary gives a plain-English briefing of what happened on the board in that period.

  • Custom prompt: At the bottom of Settings, add an optional prompt to shape the summary, for example “Summarise as a daily briefing”.

Dashboard extras

  • Date filters: Show only cards created “This week” or “Last week” to spot recent trends.
  • Digital clock: Show a large clock in the top-left corner, ideal for wall screens.
  • Themes: Switch between light and dark, or follow your system setting.
  • Clear local storage: To start over, use “Clear local storage” in the Other tab. This erases every saved configuration.

Sharing your configuration

Share your dashboard setup so colleagues don’t have to build it from scratch.

Share a link

Click “Share configuration” to create a unique link. When a colleague opens it, Trellops loads your exact layout: board IDs, blocks and settings.

Export to a file

To keep everything local, choose “Export configuration to a file”. Your colleague can load it with “Import”.
