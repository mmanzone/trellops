At a glance

Trellops is built to show your Trello data, not to collect it. Here is the short version; the details follow.

Stays in your browser

  • Your dashboard layout, colours and settings (on the Free plan)
  • Cached map locations

Kept on our server

  • Your account record: Trello member ID, username, name, email, plan, the IDs of boards you set up, and when you signed in
  • On Pro, your dashboard settings, so they follow you to other devices

Leaves your browser only when you ask

  • A layout skeleton, when you share a link
  • Addresses, sent to Google Maps for lookup
  • Card contents, sent to Google’s Gemini when you request an AI summary

The “local first” approach

Trellops is a client-side application. For almost every feature, your data never leaves your browser. We do not track what you do in the app, sell your data, or store your Trello card contents on our servers. The only thing we keep about you is a small account record, described below.

Data storage

Your dashboard configuration (such as block names, tile colours and layout settings) is stored in your browser’s local storage, so your dashboard is ready whenever you open Trellops.

Exception: sharing a configuration

If you choose to use “Share configuration via link”, we briefly store a skeleton of your layout on our server so the link works for your colleagues. This includes:

  • Trello board IDs
  • Block names and layout order
  • Home address coordinates, if set
We do not store card data, card descriptions or personal information (PII) as part of sharing.

The person who created a link can revoke it at any time.

Exception: Pro settings saved to your account

On the Pro plan, each board's dashboard configuration (the same content as an exported configuration file, including a home address if you set one) is also saved to your account on our server, so your dashboards are ready on any device. On the Free plan, settings stay in your browser only.

Your account record

When you sign in, Trellops checks your Trello sign-in with Trello once and keeps a short record of your account, so we can link plans and support requests to you. The record holds:

  • Your Trello member ID, username and full name
  • The email address on your Trello account
  • When you first signed in, when you last used Trellops, and how many times you have signed in
  • The IDs of the Trello boards you set up in Trellops (not their names or contents)
  • Your plan (Free, Pro, trial or Board plan) and the boards it covers

If you ask for a free plan for a charity or NGO, we also keep what you write in that form: the organisation's name, type and location, how you plan to use Trellops, and why you need the free plan.

Your Trello token is used for that check and is never stored on our server. We set one cookie, trellops_session, which holds a signed copy of your member ID for 30 days so our server knows it is you. Board names and card contents are not part of the record. To have your record deleted, contact us.

Trello permissions: read and write

When you sign in, Trellops asks for access to your Trello boards. That access is limited to the features you use.

Default: read-only

By default Trellops only asks for read permission. Lists and cards are downloaded only to show them on your dashboard. Trellops cannot change, delete or create anything.

Your email address

Trellops also asks Trello for account permission, which lets us read the email address on your Trello account for your account record. It does not give access to your password or let Trellops change your account.

Optional: write access

Some Map features need write permission. You are asked for it only when you try to:

  1. Move a card on the map: drag a card from one list to another in the Map view.
  2. Save coordinates: store the latitude and longitude found from a card’s address on the card itself, which speeds up future loads.

Third-party services

Some features rely on these services:

  • Google Maps Platform: If a card has an address in its description (rather than coordinates), that address text is sent to Google Maps to turn it into map coordinates (geocoding). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API): When you use AI summaries on the Statistics page, the contents of the cards in the selected period (including descriptions, comments and members) are sent securely to Google’s Gemini models to produce the summary. See the Google API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  • Paddle: Paid plans are sold by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. When you buy a plan, Paddle collects your payment details, billing address and email, and handles tax, receipts and refunds. Trellops receives your subscription status, plan, renewal date and a Paddle customer ID, never your card details. See the Paddle Privacy Policy.
  • Resend: When you sign in for the first time, or send a charity request, the Trellops administrators get an email through Resend. It holds your Trello username, name, email address and member ID, and for a charity request the details you entered and the board ID (never the board name or cards). See the Resend Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us through our support channels.
