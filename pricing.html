        Good to know

        Do I need a card to start?
        No. Free needs only your Trello account. People who used Trellops before paid plans open get a 14-day Pro trial.
        Pro or Board plan?
        Pro is for one person and follows you across up to 5 boards (more with packs). A Board plan is bought once for a board and covers everyone who is a member of it, which is cheaper for a team.
        Why is the fastest refresh one minute?
        Trello limits how often all Trellops dashboards together can ask for data. A one-minute floor keeps every dashboard working. You can still press Refresh for live numbers.
        Taxes and currency
        Prices are in US dollars. Payments are handled by our reseller, which shows local currency and adds any sales tax at checkout. Cancel at any time; access continues to the end of the paid period.

        Typical use cases
        • Free: a volunteer coordinator keeping an eye on one board's backlog from a laptop.
        • Pro: a project manager with several client boards who wants the map, trends and a wall display that refreshes every minute.
        • Board plan: an operations team with a shared board on a TV in the office, where everyone can open the same dashboard.
        • Board plan, free for charities: an emergency-service or community group running their call-outs on one board.
